## What the current measurements can estimate

The smoke used only the first eight MT-Bench questions, two turns, T=0,32 requested output tokens and one timing repetition. Its measured target-only rates were roughly32–39 comparison tokens/s per GPU; speculative rates were roughly77–91. If every full-panel generation reached512 tokens and these short-run rates carried over, the22,400 measured generations would require roughly8 hours of wall time on eight GPUs (about64 GPU-hours), plus warmup and startup. Early termination can reduce this; longer contexts, temperatures and workload differences can increase it. Treat this as a scheduling estimate, not a promised duration or a benchmark result. A second full node can reduce wall time without changing the registered repetitions or coverage.

Two identical released drafters matched greedy decoding in the pooled engineering check and offered no new candidate diversity on that prompt. Distinct trained specialists and held-out calibration are therefore the meaningful next pooling test. The prototype currently includes sequential drafting and host pooling; its single-prompt time is not a production serving result.
